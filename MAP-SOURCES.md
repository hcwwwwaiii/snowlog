Japan: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 – States, Provinces, version in the official nvkelso/natural-earth-vector repository fetched 2026-10-14. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson

47 Japanese prefectures were selected. The presentation crops to the main islands (129–146 E, 30.2–45.7 N), simplifies paths to 0.45 SVG units and omits shapes below one square SVG unit. Okinawa and some minor islands are outside this view. Six Tohoku prefectures share a destination group; the Kansai destination currently highlights Shiga only, where the listed venue is located. Colored areas indicate destination groups in this curated directory, not ski slope boundaries. Pins are representative regional locations, not resort entrances.
